About

Bell Consulting is the architect of structural advantage.

Origin

A Consultancy Forged in the Next Economy.

From our base in Amsterdam, a city built on foresight and global trade, Bell Consulting was formed to correct a fundamental flaw in modern advisory. We saw a landscape of consultancies too slow to act, too theoretical to implement and too siloed to see the entire picture. We engineered a different model; one built for velocity and grounded in the commercial realities of a market that waits for no one. Our forward-looking stance is an active discipline, focused on decoding the second and third-order consequences of disruption to give our clients a definitive structural advantage.

Our Mission

To Engineer Market Dominance

Bell Consulting exists to dismantle the gap between strategic ambition and commercial reality. We fuse rigorous insight with creative fearlessness to ensure our clients don’t just navigate disruption but actively weaponise it. We rewrite the rules of industry to favour those bold enough to act. We are here to make your business faster, smarter and more enduring than your competition.

Our Vision

The End of Siloed Thinking

We foresee a business landscape where the distinction between internal culture and external brand dissolves into a single, powerful velocity. We are building the consultancy that the next decade demands. A consultancy that proves integration is the only path to sustainable speed. We aim to define the standard for how modern businesses grow, proving that profit and purpose are not competing forces, but accelerants for one another.

Our Culture

The Human Engine

At Bell Consulting, our culture is defined by the individuals who build it. We hire complex, multifaceted human beings rather than just resumes. Whether they are artists, athletes, parents or explorers, our team brings their full selves to the table. This freedom to be authentic fuels the radical thinking our client’s demand. We are rewriting the future of business while building a community that champions individuality whilst enjoying the journey.

Diversity is Our Strategic Advantage

We commit to amplifying every voice, fostering rigorous mentorship and driving diversity across our Amsterdam headquarters and global operations.

Women

Bell Consulting aggressively champions the advancement of women in leadership. We invest in the mentorship required to shatter glass ceilings and ensure Bell Consulting remains the premier destination for ambitious female talent.

Pride

We build a workspace where LGBTQ+ colleagues lead with authenticity. Through active community engagement and education, we ensure that inclusion is a lived reality rather than just a policy.

Perspectives of People of Colour

We actively support the professional elevation of our colleagues of colour. Bell Consulting focuses on cultivating a deep sense of belonging and leadership opportunity through dedicated cultural and community initiatives.

Bi-Cultural Heritage

We celebrate the Turkish, Moroccan and Indonesian roots that define modern Amsterdam. We view this unique cultural mosaic not just as history, but as a source of diverse perspective. We provide the platform and pathways for bi-cultural talent to ascend to the highest levels of our firm.

Faith & Belief

We commit to respecting the spiritual lives of our team. In a secular business world, Bell Consulting creates space for the values, prayer and holidays that matter to our religious colleagues. We believe that spiritual grounding fosters the resilience and empathy required for bold leadership.

Our Impact

The Engine for Change

Accepting the current state of the world is a failure of imagination. Bell Consulting refuses to endorse a status quo that limits potential, whether for businesses, communities or the planet. We deploy our strategic firepower to dismantle systemic barriers and architect a future that is more equitable and sustainable. We partner with the organisations and movements that are doing the hard work on the ground, amplifying their ability to force meaningful progress.

Core Focus Areas
Systemic Equality

We do not just advocate for fairness; we engineer platforms that elevate underserved voices. We fight to dismantle the structural inequities that prevent talent from rising.

Economic Mobility

Potential is evenly distributed; opportunity is not. We focus on initiatives that secure the foundational needs required for individuals to ascend the economic ladder and build a stable future.

Active Sustainability

Environmental protection is not a passive goal. We take decisive action to safeguard the planet, treating ecological resilience as a critical component of long-term economic viability.
