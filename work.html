Case Studies: Rewriting the Rules
Financial Services · Frankfurt, Germany · Organisation & Culture + Brand Building

The Banking Disruptor

The Problem

A legacy German retail bank was losing market share to agile fintech competitors. Their internal culture was rigid, siloed and risk-averse, resulting in a product launch cycle that was three times slower than the market average. Their brand was perceived as stable but stagnant, failing to attract younger demographics.

The Action

Bell Consultancy deployed a “Culture as a Catalyst” program to dismantle internal hierarchies. We restructured their product teams into cross-functional agile squads, giving them autonomous decision rights. Simultaneously, we refreshed the brand architecture to signal a shift from “custodian of wealth” to “partner in progress,” launching a new visual identity that mirrored this agility.

We then operationalised this shift by embedding new governance frameworks that decentralised approval processes for low-risk features, removing the bureaucratic drag that had stifled innovation. By aligning incentive structures with team-based delivery metrics rather than individual KPIs, we fostered a culture of rapid experimentation and collective ownership, ensuring the new brand promise was substantiated by actual product velocity.

The Result
  • 3x faster speed-to-market for new digital features (reduced from 9 months to 3 months).
  • 18% uplift in new account openings among the under-35 demographic within six months.
  • Employee engagement scores rose by 14 points, with “empowerment” metrics reaching an all-time high.
Fashion Retail · Stockholm, Sweden · Business Strategy + Experience & Innovation

The Sustainable Retail Pivot

The Problem

A prominent fast-fashion retailer faced an existential threat: declining footfall and a reputational crisis regarding sustainability. Their existing business model relied on high product volume and low profit margin, a strategy that was becoming increasingly toxic to their core customer base in Scandinavia.

The Action

We engineered a radical “Circular Growth” strategy. We helped them launch a dedicated resale and repair vertical within their flagship stores, turning a sustainability liability into a new revenue stream. We redesigned the in-store experience to focus on community and longevity rather than just transaction volume, integrating digital tools to track the lifecycle of every garment.

To support this model, Bell Consulting restructured their supply chain logic, shifting focus from pure unit cost reduction to “lifecycle value capture.” We developed a proprietary pricing algorithm for the resale vertical that balanced inventory velocity with margin protection, ensuring the new business unit could operate profitably independent of the main line. This required retraining store staff as “garment custodians,” fundamentally changing the customer interaction model.

The Result
  • New revenue stream generated €4.2M in its first year.
  • Customer retention rates increased by 8% among high-value loyalty members.
  • Brand sentiment improved by 12 percentage points in key sustainability metrics within 12 months.
Manufacturing / B2B Tech · Lyon, France · Market Excellence + Go-to-Market Innovation

The Industrial Tech Transformation

The Problem

A leading French industrial component manufacturer had developed a groundbreaking IoT sensor but was failing to commercialise it. Their sales force was engineered to sell hardware commodities, not complex software-enabled solutions. They were trapped in a “cost-plus” pricing model that left millions in value on the table.

The Action

We rebuilt their go-to-market engine from the ground up. Bell Consulting segmented the market based on “value-at-risk” rather than company size, targeting clients where downtime was most expensive. We retrained the sales leadership on value-based selling and implemented a dynamic pricing model that captured a share of the efficiency gains the sensors delivered.

Crucially, we re-architected the sales enablement process, creating a suite of ROI calculators and diagnostic tools that allowed sales reps to quantify the financial impact of the solution in real-time. We aligned the marketing and sales funnels to ensure that lead qualification criteria focused on technical readiness and budget authority, drastically reducing wasted effort on low-probability prospects. This precision targeting allowed the sales team to move from transactional vendor relationships to strategic consultancy roles.

The Result
  • Average deal size increased by 28% in the first two quarters.
  • Sales cycle reduced by 4 weeks due to clearer value articulation.
  • Captured 9% market share in the target segment within 18 months of the re-launch.
